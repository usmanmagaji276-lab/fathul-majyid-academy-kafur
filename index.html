About Our School

Fathul Majyid Academy Kafur is an educational institution located in Kafur Local Government Area, Katsina State, Nigeria. The school provides education from Nursery and Primary through Junior and Senior levels.

Our Educational Sections

Nursery

Building a strong foundation for young learners.

Primary

Developing knowledge, skills and good character.

Junior Secondary

Preparing students for higher education and life.

Senior Secondary

Preparing students for academic and future careers.

Our Vision

To provide quality education that combines academic excellence with sound moral and Islamic values.

Our Mission

To nurture knowledgeable, disciplined and responsible learners who can contribute positively to society.
