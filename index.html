About us

Education rooted in character and purpose

Fathul Majyid Academy Kafur is an educational institution dedicated to helping learners develop knowledge, discipline, and strong Islamic moral values.

The school supports students from Nursery and Primary through Junior and Senior levels, creating a learning journey that balances academic development with personal responsibility and character formation.
